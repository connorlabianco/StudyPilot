Your Canvas courses, turned into a study coach.

StudyPilot plans your week, turns your lectures into practice quizzes, and tells you exactly what to study before each exam. It works with the AI you already use.

Add to Chrome, it's free No account, no API key, no subscription.

Everything a semester needs, in one panel

StudyPilot sits next to Canvas and reads your own courses with your normal Canvas login.

Your week at a glance

Every deadline across your courses, what changed since you last looked, and exam dates found in your syllabus and announcements.

Practice from your lectures

Your AI writes questions from your slides and recordings. You take them right next to Canvas, with real math and code.

Know what you've learned

Topic coverage and mastery levels for every lecture. Missed questions come back until they stick.

Exam countdown

What the exam covers, how ready you are, and what to do today, ramping up to timed practice exams.

Focus timer

Start with just 5 minutes, or 25+5 and 50+10 rounds. It keeps running with the panel closed and tracks your week.

Search everything

Slides by page, recordings by the minute, assignments, announcements and syllabi, searched on your own computer.

Learn from graded work

Your AI reads your graded Gradescope copies and quizzes you on exactly where you lost points.

Grades that make sense

A grade calculator with weights read straight from your syllabus.

Files, tidied

Course files, your submissions and full backups as neat zips, organized the way your course is.

Private and honest by design

No StudyPilot serverYour data stays in your browser, plus an optional private sync folder in your own Google Drive.
Read-only on CanvasIt never submits anything, and never opens quizzes, tests or locked items.
Built for learningIt helps you understand the material. It won't do graded work for you.
