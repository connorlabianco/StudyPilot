StudyPilot Support

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub, or email connoralabianco@gmail.com. Please include your school's Canvas address (like yourschool.instructure.com), what you clicked, and what happened. Never include passwords, access tokens or screenshots of your grades.

Common questions

StudyPilot doesn't find my Canvas.

Open Canvas in a tab and log in; StudyPilot connects automatically. If your school's Canvas address doesn't end in instructure.com (for example canvas.yourschool.edu), choose "Type your Canvas address instead" and allow access when Chrome asks.

Something I turned in still shows as due.

Canvas doesn't see work turned in on Gradescope or on paper until it's graded. Connect Gradescope (Settings › Connections) so StudyPilot can check, or click the check mark next to the item to mark it done.

How do quizzes work without an API key?

StudyPilot prepares a request from your lecture and opens your AI with it filled in. You press Send, then copy the reply; StudyPilot notices it and imports the quiz. You take the quiz inside StudyPilot.

My AI's reply didn't import.

Make sure you copied the whole reply, including the code block. If your AI added extra text, ask it to "reply with only the studypilot-quiz block", then copy again. You can also paste it in Practice › "Add questions you got elsewhere".

Search doesn't find things inside my slides.

Search looks inside lectures StudyPilot has read. In the search window, click "Read the other … module items", or turn on background prep in Settings › Data & privacy.

Can StudyPilot do my homework or answer quiz questions?

No. It never opens quizzes or tests, and it's built to help you learn the material, not to complete graded work.

How do I delete my data?

Settings › Data & privacy › Clear all StudyPilot data, or remove the extension. If you used Google sign-in, see the privacy policy for removing the synced copy.
