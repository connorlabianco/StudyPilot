      How it's calculated

      Weighted grade = (w₁×g₁ + w₂×g₂ + …) ÷ (w₁ + w₂ + …). When weights add up to 100%, that's just the sum of each weight times its grade. Rows with no weight count equally. In Points mode each row's grade is earned ÷ possible, weighted by points possible. Letters use the scale A+ 97–100, A 93–96, A− 90–92, B+ 87–89, B 83–86, B− 80–82, C+ 77–79, C 73–76, C− 70–72, D+ 67–69, D 63–66, D− 60–62, F below 60; a letter entry counts as the middle of its band (F counts as 50).
